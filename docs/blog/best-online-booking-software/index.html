It's 9:47 PM. A new customer finds your business, decides they want to book… and your only option is "call us during business hours."

By morning, they've booked with someone else. You never even knew they existed.

That's the quiet cost of not having online booking. Customers book when they are ready, and more and more, that's evenings and weekends. The good news: fixing it costs anywhere from $0 to a few dollars a month, and you can be live this afternoon.

I build custom booking systems for clients, and I'll still tell you the same thing I'd tell a friend: start with one of these tools. Here's how to pick the right one.

The feature that pays for itself: reminders

Automatic reminders are the simplest way to cut no-shows. A single missed appointment often costs more than a year of a basic plan. When you compare tools, check which plan includes SMS reminders (not just email), because text messages are harder to miss.

What actually matters when choosing

  1. Meetings or appointments? Calls and consultations suit Calendly or Cal.com. Services with staff, rooms and durations suit Acuity, Setmore or SimplyBook.me.
  2. How does the price grow? Per-user pricing gets expensive as you hire. Per-plan pricing (Acuity, SimplyBook.me) stays flatter, but watch calendar and booking limits.
  3. Deposits and payments. Taking payment or a deposit at booking is one of the strongest no-show defenses. Check which payment providers each tool supports.
  4. Reminders. Email is standard. SMS is often a paid feature, and availability varies by country.
  5. Your calendar. Two-way sync with Google or Outlook prevents double bookings.
  6. Client data. Booking tools store names, phone numbers and sometimes health information. If that applies to you, look for a data processing agreement and compliance options (for example, HIPAA in US healthcare, GDPR for EU customers).
warning

Common mistakes to avoid

  • No buffer time between appointments. Add travel or cleanup time, or your day will run late.
  • Letting clients book too far ahead (or too last-minute). Set minimum notice and maximum advance booking windows.
  • No cancellation policy. Write one, show it at booking, and back it with a deposit if no-shows hurt.
  • Hiding the booking link. Put it on your website, Google Business Profile, Instagram bio and email signature.
  • Forgetting time zones. If clients are abroad, make sure times display in their time zone.

When off-the-shelf booking isn't enough

These tools cover most businesses. A custom booking system starts to make sense when:

  • Your booking rules are unusual (multi-step services, equipment or room juggling, complex pricing)
  • Booking needs to live inside your own app, portal or membership site
  • You're connecting bookings to a CRM, inventory, or custom reporting
  • Per-user or per-booking costs are climbing as you grow

FAQ

What is the best free online booking software?expand_more

Setmore's free plan is very generous for small teams (up to 4 users, unlimited appointments). Calendly's free plan works well for one type of meeting, Cal.com is free for one user with unlimited event types, and SimplyBook.me is free for up to 50 bookings a month.

How much does online booking software cost?expand_more

Paid plans in this guide start at $5 per user per month (Setmore Pro) and run to about $49 per month for Acuity Premium or €49.9 per month for SimplyBook.me Premium, on annual billing. Many businesses can start on a free plan.

Which booking software is best for salons and clinics?expand_more

Look at SimplyBook.me or Acuity Scheduling. Both are built around staff, services and appointments, and both price per plan rather than per user. Setmore is a good free starting point for small teams.

Can online booking reduce no-shows?expand_more

Yes. Automatic reminders (especially SMS), clear cancellation policies and deposits at booking are the most common ways businesses reduce no-shows. Check which plan includes each feature.

Is Calendly good for appointment-based businesses?expand_more

Calendly shines for meetings and calls. For services with multiple staff, resources and durations, appointment-focused tools like Acuity, Setmore or SimplyBook.me are usually a better fit.

When should I build a custom booking system?expand_more

When your booking rules don't fit standard tools, when booking must live inside your own app or portal, or when you need it tightly connected to a CRM or other systems. Try an off-the-shelf tool first so you learn exactly what you need.

The bottom line

  • Booking calls and meetings? Calendly.
  • Client appointments, predictable pricing? Acuity Scheduling.
  • Small team, tiny budget? Setmore's free plan.

  • Want control, or building it into your product? Cal.com.
