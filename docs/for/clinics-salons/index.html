Questions

Should I just use Calendly, Acuity or Setmore?expand_more

If a standard tool fits your rules, start there, and I'll say so. Custom booking makes sense when your rules are unusual (rooms, equipment, packages), when booking must live inside your own site or app, or when it needs to connect to your CRM.

Can clients pay a deposit when they book?expand_more

Yes. I integrate payment providers like Stripe for deposits, full payments and refunds.

Will it work on phones?expand_more

Yes. It's designed mobile-first, because that's where most clients book.

How long does it take?expand_more

Most booking systems take 4–8 weeks, depending on your rules and integrations.
