Questions

Should my agency just use HubSpot or Pipedrive?expand_more

Often, yes, at first, and I'll tell you if that's the better fit. Custom makes sense when you're bending your process to fit the tool, paying for many unused seats, or need the CRM tightly connected to your own systems.

Can you import our spreadsheets and existing CRM data?expand_more

Yes. I map your fields, clean duplicates and import contacts, deals and history as part of the project.

Who owns the CRM when it's done?expand_more

You do: the code, the database and every account are in your name.

How long does a custom CRM take?expand_more

Most agency CRMs take 6–12 weeks, split into milestones so your team can start using the core pipeline early.
