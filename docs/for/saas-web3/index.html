Questions

Does a custom dashboard replace Datadog or Grafana?expand_more

No. It usually sits on top of them, using them as data sources, and adds your business context and actions in one place.

Can it connect to our database and on-chain data?expand_more

Yes. Dashboards can read from your database, monitoring APIs, chain RPCs and indexers, with read-only access where that's safer.

Who can see and do what?expand_more

Roles and permissions control which screens and actions each person has, and actions can be logged for auditing.

How long does it take?expand_more

Most dashboards and admin panels take 4–10 weeks, starting with the one screen your team needs most.
