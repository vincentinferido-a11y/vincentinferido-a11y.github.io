    Behind the Architecture

    I design and build web products from start to finish, from the first Figma concept to a production Next.js app, with smart contracts when a product needs them.

    When design, front end and back end are handled by different people, details get lost between them. Because I work across all three, I can take a product from idea to launch myself, and what ships matches what was designed. I use AI throughout that process to move faster, and I check everything it produces.

    WORKING PHILOSOPHY

    It depends on scope. A focused marketing site or landing page usually takes 1–2 weeks, and a full-stack MVP with auth, a database and a CMS usually takes 4–8 weeks. I'll give you a clear estimate after a short discovery call.
    Do you work with existing teams or build solo from scratch? expand_more
    Both. I can build a product solo from Figma to deployment, or join an existing team and fit into your codebase, design system and workflow.
    What is your billing & engagement structure? expand_more
    For freelance work I usually quote fixed-scope milestones, so you know the cost up front. I'm also open to part-time and full-time roles. We can agree on the details on a quick call.
    Can you work within our existing design system and codebase? expand_more
    Absolutely. I quickly assimilate into established Figma design systems, existing GitHub CI/CD pipelines, and monorepos (Turborepo, Nx) without disturbing your ongoing release cadence.
    Can I see the source code or license a project? expand_more
    Yes. Public projects can be browsed on GitHub. Private projects (like HiKIDAI and the CRM) can be shared with read-only access for evaluation.
