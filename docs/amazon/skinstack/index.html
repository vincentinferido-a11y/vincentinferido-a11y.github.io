SkinStack — Amazon listing & A+ content

A complete Amazon creative package for a premium skincare line: visual direction, a 9-image listing set for the hero SKU, and A+ content modules. SkinStack is a concept brand I created for this study, not a client.

  • 9-image listing set
  • A+ content
  • Mood board
  • Photoshop compositing
  • AI-assisted, manually retouched
01 · Brief

Product & positioning

Starting point: one product and a short positioning statement, the same input an agency hands over.

Product

Hyaluronic Acid Serum, 30 mL, the hero SKU in a four-serum line (Hyaluronic, Vitamin C, Niacinamide, Retinol).

Audience

Shoppers who compare ingredients and want clinical results with a premium, calm feel.

Positioning

Science-backed, clean and quietly luxurious. No loud claims, no clutter.

02 · Visual direction

Mood board

Lavender-gray light, ivory silk and pale stone give the line a soft, editorial luxury feel. Each serum has its own glass color (clear, amber, frosted, violet) so the range reads as a system at thumbnail size.

03 · Listing images

The 9-image set

Every image has one job in the shopper's decision. Click any image to view it full size.

04 · A+ content

A+ modules

Brand story, philosophy and ingredients, so the product page reads like the brand's own site.

05 · Process

AI in the workflow, finished by hand

AI speeds up scene creation. Photoshop makes the result believable. The final images are checked at 100% zoom for the usual AI tells: soft edges, garbled text, mismatched light and plastic textures.

  1. Direction

    Mood board, palette and lighting rules before any image is made.

  2. Scene generation

    AI backgrounds and props, generated against the mood board.

  3. Product & label

    One master bottle, with label art applied as a smart object so the text stays sharp.

  4. Composite

    Matched light direction, contact shadows, glass reflections and refraction.

  5. Retouch & QA

    Edge cleanup, texture fixes, and a check of every image at full size.
